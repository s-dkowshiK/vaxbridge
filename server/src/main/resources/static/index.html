Help & about

How VaxBridge works and what it does not do

VaxBridge

Reads a child's vaccination record from another country and checks it against the US catch-up schedule, so immigrant and refugee kids can enroll in school without weeks of delay or unneeded shots.

What can I upload?

Photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) or a PDF of the vaccination card, in any language. Use a clear, well-lit photo with every date visible. Several photos can be pages of one record.

Who decides what the child needs?

A school nurse or pediatrician. VaxBridge reads and translates the record and does the date math, but a licensed provider checks the original record and signs the report.

How are doses checked?

A rules engine checks every dose against the CDC catch-up schedule: minimum ages, minimum intervals, the 4-day grace period, live-vaccine spacing and the 2016 oral polio switch. Each dose that does not count shows the reason.

Why does a row say "Check"?

The AI was not sure it read that entry correctly, for example a smudged date. Compare it with the original record and fix it before continuing.

Is the record saved?

No. VaxBridge sends the photo to the AI to read it and does not store it. Print or save the report as a PDF to keep it.
